QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Everything clear from the start.

What is JengaPro?+

JengaPro is a construction management platform that brings projects, sites, teams, attendance, materials and operations into one place.

Who is JengaPro for?+

JengaPro is built for contractors and the teams running construction projects day to day.

Can I manage multiple construction projects and sites?+

Yes. JengaPro is designed to help contractors organize projects and the sites connected to them.

Can I track workers and attendance?+

Yes. Workforce assignments and daily attendance are part of the operating picture JengaPro helps you manage.

Can different team members use JengaPro?+

JengaPro supports the different people involved in construction operations, from contractors to site and accounts teams.
